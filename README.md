# Ustrój Kantyna

**A conceptual exploration of a new social order.**

Ustrój Kantyna is imagined as a utopian system that has evolved out of capitalism rather than rejected it. It does not claim to be a pure break from what came before. Instead, it treats capitalism as a temporary developmental stage and attempts to grow beyond it.

It is modeled on everything and on nothing at once.

---

## Core Characteristics

- **Needs-fundamental**  
  The primary organizing principle is the reliable satisfaction of fundamental human needs. Production, distribution, and access are structured first around what people actually require to live well, not around profit extraction or status competition.

- **Technologically integrated and dependent**  
  The system is deeply interwoven with advanced technology. It does not treat technology as an optional tool but as a structural condition of its own existence. Coordination, resource allocation, transparency, and large-scale logistics rely on continuous technological mediation.

- **Evolved, not invented from zero**  
  It inherits mechanisms, infrastructures, and knowledge from capitalist societies while attempting to redirect their purpose. Continuity and transformation are held in tension.

---

## Design Stance

This is not a finished blueprint.  
It is a space for thinking about what a post-capitalist order might look like if it took human needs as primary and technological integration as unavoidable.

Open questions include:
- How dependency on complex systems can be made resilient rather than fragile
- How needs can be defined without becoming authoritarian
- How technological mediation can increase freedom instead of narrowing it
- What forms of coordination remain human when most logistics become automated

---

## Status

Early conceptual stage.  
A place for notes, tensions, speculative architectures, and honest limits.
